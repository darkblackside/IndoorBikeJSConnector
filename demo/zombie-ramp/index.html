● Zombie Ramp ● ● ●

A ramp FTP test. After the warm-up the target rises every minute until you can't go on. In ERG mode the trainer sets the wattage for you; without it you match the target with gears and cadence. The zombies run at the target wattage: hold it and they stay behind you, fall short and they close in. Stop pedalling and the test ends. FTP = last fully completed minute × 0.75.
