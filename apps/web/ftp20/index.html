● 20-min FTP test

The classic FTP test, with a coach riding along. Your power moves you down the road; the scenery changes every few minutes. About 50 minutes in total.

  1. Warm-up, 20 min (ERG). Easy spinning, a moderate block, quick-leg spin-ups and a 2-minute opener at your hard wattage that lifts your heart rate briefly towards ~90 % of max. Then recover.
  2. The test, 20 min (no ERG). All-out, but even: as hard as you can hold for the full 20 minutes. You set the power with gears and cadence. Don't start too hot; empty the tank in the last 2–3 minutes.
  3. Cool-down, 10 min (ERG). Spin easy and let your heart rate come down.

FTP = average power of the 20 minutes × 0.95. During the test you won't see your average or an FTP estimate: pace by feel. The coach tells you whether you're above or below your own average, and gives cadence, feeling and a heart-rate guide for every block. Warm-up and cool-down can be skipped.

Easy: you could chat (about half your FTP). Hard: the 2-minute opener, around your FTP or your best guess of it. The moderate warm-up level is the midpoint. Age or max heart rate (it overrides the age) give a heart-rate guide: max HR ≈ 208 − 0.7 × age.
